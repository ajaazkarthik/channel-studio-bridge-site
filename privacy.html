CS Channel Studio BridgePRIVACY

Effective 11 November 2026

Privacy policy

Channel Studio Bridge is an owner-operated Mac desktop tool. This policy covers the local tool's handling of Google and YouTube information and its public information site.

Data accessed

With account-owner Google OAuth consent, the tool can access the selected YouTube channel's identity, video identifiers, titles, metadata, processing and privacy status, publishing schedules, comments and moderation data when requested, and YouTube Analytics metrics. It can use locally supplied video files, thumbnails and subtitle tracks.

Purpose and permissions

Data is used only to identify the correct channel, complete owner-approved video uploads or scheduling, manage owner-requested metadata or comments, and inspect reporting or performance statistics. The tool does not sell Google data, provide advertising targeting or buy engagement.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and necessary operational records are stored locally on the operator's Mac in restricted application support directories. The OAuth redirect is to localhost. This public information website does not receive Google OAuth credentials, passwords, private analytics, or video files and does not expose a public upload API.

Sharing and subprocessors

Authorized media and metadata are transmitted to Google/YouTube through YouTube API Services when the owner directs. The owner may deliberately connect an AI assistant or cloud-storage provider for specific production workflows; in that case, relevant operational context or files can be processed by those provider services under their own agreements. The tool does not sell Google data or independently disclose it to advertisers.

Retention and deletion

The operator retains local credentials, logs and receipts as needed for authorized tasks, review and troubleshooting, and may delete them. Revoke the OAuth grant via Google Account connections and remove local authorization files. Revocation stops new access but does not automatically delete YouTube videos already published.

Google user-data policies

Use of Google information is limited to approved purposes, consistent with the Google API Services User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are subject to YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

Website visitors

GitHub Pages hosts this static information site. The site has no embedded advertising or analytics scripts and does not set its own cookies. GitHub may process routine technical server logs under its policies.

Contact

Use the project issue tracker for privacy and support requests. Access can also be withdrawn directly from Google's account settings.
